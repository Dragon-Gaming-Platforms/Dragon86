Arculator emulates an Acorn Archimedes, but Dragon86 does not ship an OS for it: Arthur and RISC OS ROM images are copyrighted (the rights now sit with RISC OS Developments Ltd), so you bring your own ROM image. It is kept in this browser's storage — it never leaves your machine.

The desktop Arculator download includes RISC OS 3.1 ROMs distributed with the rights-holder's permission — the file roms/riscos311/ros311 from that archive works here. A ROM dumped from an Archimedes you own works too.
