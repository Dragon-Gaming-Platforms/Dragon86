Play games you provide. No games or BIOS files are included.

Choose a local ROM/disc image to start. PlayStation uses EmulatorJS's single-threaded pcsx_rearmed core and does not need cross-origin isolation. CHD or PBP is recommended for PS1 discs. The emulator engine is loaded from EmulatorJS's CDN; an internet connection is required.

Only load games and BIOS files you have the right to use. Files are selected from your device and are not uploaded by this frontend.
